## Cross-Border Options (Germany + UK)

Use when Bulgarian price is >15–20% higher than the EU/UK price after all fees. Always calculate **landed cost** before concluding cross-border is cheaper.

### Warranty / returns warning
Cross-border purchases complicate warranty claims. If a product fails under warranty, you typically need to ship it back to the country of purchase. For cheap items (cameras, routers <€50) this rarely makes sense — factor it in. For expensive items, check whether the brand has a Bulgarian service centre (TP-Link, Samsung, Philips do; niche brands often don't).

### UK

> ⚠️ **Post-Brexit:** The UK is outside the EU. Shipments from UK to Bulgaria may attract Bulgarian customs duties and import VAT. Low-value items (<~€150 declared) are often waved through in practice, but it is a real risk on higher-value orders. Factor this in before choosing UK over Germany.

### When cross-border makes sense

- Product is **>15–20% cheaper** in DE/UK after all fees (use landed cost formula below)
- Product is **unavailable** in Bulgaria or only on eMAG marketplace with an unknown seller
- Higher-spec variant or bundle not sold locally

### When to stay local

- Price gap is small — cross-border warranty claims are inconvenient
- You need the product within a few days
- Item is heavy or bulky (GGBG fee per kg erodes savings fast)
- UK order: customs duty risk makes the saving uncertain

### Landed cost formulas

```
Germany (direct):   Amazon.de price + DE shipping (free if ≥ €49)
Germany (forward):  German price + GGBG fee (~€2/kg × weight in kg, round up)
UK (direct):        Amazon.co.uk price + UK shipping + customs risk
UK (forward):       UK price + forwarder fee + customs risk

Net saving = Cheapest Bulgarian price − Landed cost
Only proceed cross-border if Net saving is meaningful (suggest ≥ €10 after all fees).
```
